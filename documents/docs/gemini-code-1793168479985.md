# Business Requirements Document (BRD) & Product Strategy
**Project:** Hybrid Multi-Vendor B2B (Wholesale) & B2C (Retail) Marketplace Platform  
**Target Market:** Consumer Retail, Boutique Resellers, Salons, Verified Factories & Importers  
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
This platform is a unified dual-market e-commerce engine designed to bridge factory-direct wholesale sourcing with mainstream retail consumer sales. The system allows verified sellers and manufacturers to liquidate inventory across two independent channels from a single catalog interface while strictly safeguarding wholesale margins, minimum order thresholds, and vendor trade secrets.

---

## 2. Core Stakeholders & User Personas

### 2.1 Retail Buyer (B2C End-Consumer)
* **Goal:** Direct purchase of items in individual units (1, 2, 3...) at fair market retail pricing.
* **Experience:** Traditional e-commerce flow (Hero banner, visual search, cart, 1-click checkout, home delivery, parcel tracking).
* **Restrictions:** Must never see wholesale trade prices, tiered discount brackets, or vendor minimum order quantities.

### 2.2 Wholesale Buyer (B2B Reseller / Business Owner)
* **Goal:** Bulk inventory procurement at discounted factory rates with predictable Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ).
* **Experience:** Business profile onboarding with trade documentation, access to wholesale catalog, bulk multi-variation matrix ordering, commercial tax invoices, and scheduled freight delivery.
* **Restrictions:** Cannot purchase below minimum volume thresholds; locked out of single-unit retail checkout rates.

### 2.3 Seller / Vendor (Manufacturer, Importer, Brand)
* **Goal:** Centralized inventory distribution across retail and wholesale customer bases.
* **Experience:** Multi-channel product listing form (`Retail`, `Wholesale`, or `Both`), stock reservation controls, sub-order fulfillment management, consignment tracking entry, and escrow payout withdrawals.
* **Restrictions:** Internal buying cost (`buy_price`) is strictly private to the seller's analytics.

### 2.4 Platform Super Admin
* **Goal:** Platform governance, merchant verification, dispute mediation, category curation, dynamic status configuration, and commission settlement.
* **Experience:** Full administration panel powered by Filament PHP for system-wide auditing, payout disbursements, and commission adjustments.

## 3. Dynamic Business Rules & Commercial Logic

### 3.1 Pricing Integrity & Channel Scoping
1. **The "Both" Sales Channel:** A seller listing a product under the `Both` channel designates the item as purchasable in single units by retail consumers and in bulk lots by business buyers.
2. **Server-Side Data Masking:** Wholesale pricing and MOQ fields are scrubbed at the database query and API serialization layers. Retail clients and unauthenticated visitors cannot inspect wholesale prices through browser dev tools or response payloads.
3. **Gated Wholesale Discovery:** Unauthenticated visitors can view public retail products. Wholesale-only items display a teaser banner: *"Wholesale Available (MOQ: X) - Register a Business Account to View Rates."*

### 3.2 Dynamic Commission & Settlement Engine
* **Commission Deductions:** Platform fees are calculated per sub-order line item upon payment authorization:
  * **Retail Orders:** Default 8% to 12% variable take-rate.
  * **Wholesale Bulk Orders:** Default 3% to 5% volume-tier take-rate.
* **Escrow Holding Window:** All vendor funds are placed into an `escrow_held_balance` ledger upon customer payment.
* **Disbursement Trigger:** Funds shift to `available_balance` 48 hours after courier status changes to `delivered`, provided no formal dispute or return ticket is raised.

### 3.3 Dynamic System Lookups (Zero Hardcoding Policy)
All operational statuses (Order Status, Payment Status, Fulfillment Milestones, Attributes, Units, Sales Channels) must reside in database lookup tables managed via the Admin Panel. No business logic can rely on hardcoded string enums in application code.
