## Behaviour changes the owner should know about

- **Plan change.** The old plan keeps running until the new plan's bill is paid. When it is paid, the new plan starts that day. Unused days of the old plan are **not** credited; proration needs an owner decision.
- **Choosing a plan again.** It voids the earlier unpaid plan-change bill.
- **Admin offline payment.** It now renews the subscription period. Before, the bill was marked paid but the plan stayed expired. The recorded amount is still not compared with the bill total; that is MON-6 in Wave 1.
- **Audit pruning.** `orders`, `sellers`, `settings` and `kyc` history is also protected, which is stricter than first planned. Adjust in `config/audit.php` if wanted.
- **Lifting a suspension.** A suspension the admin imposed (policy or manual) is only lifted with the admin's Restore button, even if the seller pays. A billing suspension is lifted automatically only once no subscription bill is left unpaid. If a seller switches to another plan, the old plan's unpaid renewal bill is voided.
- **Reclassify command after later reversals.** If a journal that was already corrected is reversed later, run `accounting:reclassify-shipping` again so the reversal is corrected too.
- **Customer refunds after PAY-1.** They appear as *pending* Refund rows for the finance team to pay out, exactly like other cancellations.

## Behaviour changes the owner should know about

- **New products wait for review.** A seller who shows a new product sends it to the admin queue. Buyers see it only after approval. A rejected product shows the reason and can be re-sent.
- **Price, channel and category changes on live products wait for review.** Buyers keep paying the current prices meanwhile. Stock and title changes stay instant.
- **A newer change replaces the waiting one.** The earlier request is kept in history as "Replaced by a newer price". Nothing is ever deleted.
- **Flash sales show approved products only.** A product waiting for review that was already enrolled in a sale stays off the sale pages until it is approved; a seller cannot enrol an unapproved product.
- **Wishlist.** A saved product that is not on sale right now shows as "No longer available", with only a Remove button.
- **Existing products were approved automatically on deploy**, dated at their creation. The moderation page has a "never reviewed" filter that lists them, in case you want to review the old catalog.
- **Refused publishing saves nothing.** When the plan limit or a suspension stops a seller from showing a product, none of that edit is saved. The seller is told to untick "Visible" to save a draft.
- **Who reviews.** Only `super_admin` can open the admin panel, so only they can reach the queues; see the open owner decision in the Deployment Guide.

## Behaviour changes the owner should know about (Wave 1)

- **A missing account stops the action (ACC-6).** If someone removes an account from the chart of accounts, payouts, refund payments or billing payments that need it are refused with a message naming it, instead of going through without a journal. Run the chart-of-accounts seeder or add the account, then retry.
- **Posted journals are permanent (ACC-9).** Corrections are made by posting a reversal, never by editing or deleting. This now holds even for direct database queries.
- **Reverse is for manual journals only (ACC-5).** To undo a system journal, undo the business event it records (cancel the order, refund, reject the payout); the journals follow automatically.
- **Manual journals (UX-ACC-1).** Admins can now post journals by hand from **Accounting → Journal Entries → New manual journal**, for example bank deposits, opening balances or corrections. A posted manual journal can't be edited; to correct one, reverse it and post the right one.
- **Courier and gateway settlements (ACC-7).** When a courier pays out COD cash, or money is moved from bKash or Nagad to the bank, record it from **Journal Entries → Courier remittance / Gateway transfer**. The books then show what couriers and gateways still owe, and their charges appear as expenses.
- **Limits (ACC-7).** A recorded settlement can't be undone from the panel yet; if one is wrong, correct it with a manual journal and tell the developer which parcels to unmark. Courier charges for prepaid parcels billed separately (not deducted from a COD payment) are entered as a manual expense journal.
- **Self-delivery and waivers (ACC-3, ACC-4, ACC-8).** These fixes apply to new events only. Past self-collected COD with a coupon, past switches to self-delivery, and past fee waivers keep their old journals. The seller reconciliation page shows any seller whose wallet and books disagree; such gaps can be closed with manual journals, or with a one-off correction command if there are many.
- **Waiver income (ACC-8).** Subscription revenue in the reports no longer includes waived fees. Earlier waivers still show as revenue with an equal promotional expense; net profit was never affected.
- **Commission timing (ACC-11).** Commission on bKash, Nagad and card orders now counts as income when the parcel is delivered, not when the payment is verified. Until then the balance sheet shows it under Deferred Commission Revenue. Orders paid before this deploy keep the commission they already booked, so commission income will look lower for a few days after deploy while new orders are in transit.
